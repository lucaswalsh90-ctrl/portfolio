# Porch Beacon

Summer 2026. Product manager and builder.

## What it is

A two-sided marketplace that matched older homeowners with caregivers. The idea was that a caregiver could trade help around the home for a place to live.

## What I built

![Porch Beacon matches screen with demo data](../assets/porch-beacon-matches.png)

*A recreation of the matches screen with made-up people.*


I built it with AI tools. It had onboarding, background checks, mutual matching and paid subscriptions. It was version controlled on GitHub and deployed live on Railway.

## What the evidence said

After launch I researched competitors, unit economics and employment law. I found that federal wage law (the FLSA) treats trading housing for care as employment. That broke the model.

## The decision

I shut it down instead of pushing ahead. I wrote up the analysis as a case study.

## What I learned

Check the rules that could kill a model before scaling it. Building fast is useful, but knowing when to stop matters just as much.
