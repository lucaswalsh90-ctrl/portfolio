# Replicator Method

Proto Hologram, summer 2026. Business development and sales intern.

## What it is

An AI sales workflow I built for the sales team by directing Claude and Apollo. It turns customers the company has already closed into new sales targets.

## How it works

It runs on a schedule. Each run:

1. Starts from a customer the company has already closed.
2. Finds similar companies, the lookalikes.
3. Finds contacts at those companies and verifies them.
4. Writes a tailored outreach angle for each account.
5. Drafts the outreach email with supporting proof points.
6. Syncs the leads into HubSpot.

A person reviews every email before it goes out. Nothing is sent automatically.

## How I improved it

I met with the CTO and the sales reps twice a week. We looked at which drafts the reps used and which they skipped, and I changed the workflow after those meetings.

## What I learned

Clean CRM data matters as much as good emails. If the records are messy, reps stop trusting the pipeline.
